About the City

Haldwani is an important city of Uttarakhand known as the gateway to the Kumaon region.

Culture

The city reflects the diverse culture and traditions of the Kumaon region.

Nature

Haldwani is surrounded by beautiful Himalayan landscapes and is close to several natural attractions.

Travel

Haldwani provides convenient access to many destinations in Uttarakhand.
